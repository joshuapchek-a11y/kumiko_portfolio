Forbidden Fruit

2023
Earthenware, Chain, Ribbon
Forbidden Fruit（禁断の果実）explores queer inequalities and restrictions depicted through the luxury fruit industry of Japan. Combined with aspects of 'Shunga' art. Luxury fruits are gifted in Japan as a sign of deep respect, loyalty and appreciation. I found this contradiction verytelling of the priorities of Japan. Growing up half Japanese in Australia I tend to compare the two countries socially and politically. This installation is also a showcase of my appreciation for my two cultures as well as the difficulties I have avoided as a queer woman by growing up in Australia.
