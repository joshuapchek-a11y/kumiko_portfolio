About+CV
Kumiko Delaney b. 2001 is a Japanese-Australian artist living on Gadigal Land in Sydney, NSW.


Through ceramic and scratch-built model mediums, Kumiko explores her culture, sexuality, the body and mind through a nostalgic and playful lens. Particularly in an increasingly digital world, she finds hand-made generations and tangible imaginations to be an imperative.
